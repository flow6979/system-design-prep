**In one line:** "Tell me about yourself" is not a resume reading but a 60-90 second pitch: Present, Past, Why this role.

- **Structure:** Present (20-30s: role, scale, highlight), Past (20-30s: relevant work plus a number), Why this role (15-20s).
- **Length:** 60-90 seconds, never over 2 minutes.
- **Interviewer checks:** communication, relevance, hooks (what to ask next), motivation.
- **Hook:** include an interesting project or number they will want to ask about.
- **Ending:** give a clear signal: "...which is why I'm excited about this role."
- **Templates:** separate ones for fresher, 2-4 years, 5+ years; widen scope with seniority.
- **Positive framing:** no negative leaving reasons; say "I want more scope".
- **Tech list:** no buzzword dump; tie tech to real work.
- **Company research:** keep "why this role" specific, not generic.

**Say in the interview:** "Right now I'm a [role] working on [work at scale]. Before that, [one relevant highlight]. And I want this role because [specific reason]."

**Avoid:** Reading the resume from class 10, personal life details, "no growth at my company", a 3-minute monologue, a memorised tone.
