**In one line:** 80% of behavioral questions are variants of 12; keep one real story and its "signal" ready for each.

- **Conflict with teammate:** signal is collaboration; story is a design or code review disagreement.
- **Disagree with manager:** backbone plus commit; push back with data, then commit fully.
- **Biggest failure:** self-awareness; a project that went wrong and what you learned.
- **A mistake:** honesty plus a process fix; a prod bug you introduced.
- **Tight deadline:** prioritisation; scope cut and delivery of the core.
- **Ownership beyond role:** you picked up an orphan problem and finished it.
- **Ambiguous requirements:** you created clarity; a vague ticket became a shipped v1.
- **Tough feedback:** growth; feedback you actually applied.
- **Production incident:** calm under pressure; mitigate, root cause, permanent fix, numbers.
- **Proudest project / learning quickly:** depth and curiosity.
- **Why leaving / why us:** motivation; positive, company-specific, 30-45 seconds.

**Say in the interview:** "Can I take a minute to think of the most relevant example?" (better than a random story).

**Avoid:** Having no story for a question or reusing one story repeatedly; blaming anyone in the leaving answer.
