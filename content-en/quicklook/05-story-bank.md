**In one line:** 6-8 prepared stories cover 90% of behavioral questions; a story bank maps each story to the questions and LPs it fits.

- **Table columns:** headline, questions/LPs covered, numbers, follow-up-ready points.
- **Picking:** list 15-20 items from the last 2-3 years with role, one number, what was hard; drop any missing one.
- **Coverage:** pick 6-8 stories, every category at least once; map 3-4 questions/LPs to each.
- **Gap check:** run through the 12 common questions; if one has no story, find a new one.
- **Practice:** say each story in 60-120 seconds and have a friend ask follow-ups.
- **Ask the interviewer:** HM round: 6-month expectations; peer round: code and design review process; bar raiser: culture.
- **Day before:** remember each headline plus number, say "tell me about yourself" twice with a timer, 2 lines on "why this company".
- **On the day:** join 10 min early, pen and paper, every answer 60-120 seconds, clarify unclear questions.
- **After each round:** spend 2 min noting which stories you used.

**Say in the interview:** "I'd love to understand what success looks like in this role in the first six months."

**Avoid:** Saying "no questions, everything is clear" (it is scored too); telling stories you never practiced out loud.
