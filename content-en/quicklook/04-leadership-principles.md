**In one line:** Big companies score the behavioral round against their own values (Amazon's 16 Leadership Principles are the most explicit); one story can cover several LPs.

- **Amazon loop:** 4-5 rounds, each ~15-20 min LP plus ~40 min coding/design.
- **Bar raiser:** a trained interviewer from another team who probes one story for ~15 min.
- **No repeats:** do not repeat a story across rounds; interviewers share notes.
- **Customer Obsession:** how you measured the customer's pain (tickets, metrics) and what changed.
- **Ownership:** you took a problem with no owner to the end; a long-term fix.
- **Dive Deep:** logs, traces, numbers; 2-3 hypotheses you rejected.
- **Bias for Action:** a reversible decision with incomplete information, plus a rollback plan.
- **Disagree and Commit:** push back with data, then commit fully, no "I told you so".
- **Earn Trust:** deliver bad news early, admit mistakes, repair the relationship.
- **Learn and Be Curious:** structured learning, applied it, shared it with the team.
- **Other companies:** Google (Googleyness), Meta, Microsoft (growth mindset), Indian product firms; the format is still STAR.

**Say in the interview:** "I raised my concern with data, but once the decision was made I committed fully."

**Avoid:** Hunting a new story for every LP (map one story to several) or repeating the same story across rounds.
