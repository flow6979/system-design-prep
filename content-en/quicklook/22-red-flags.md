**In one line:** In HLD interviews people fail on approach mistakes more than missing knowledge: avoid these 10 red flags.

- **Skipping requirements:** jumping to design solves the wrong problem. Ask for 3–5 min, FR + NFR, out of scope.
- **Over-engineering:** 12 microservices at 1K QPS. Simple v1 first, evolve only when a number demands it.
- **Buzzwords:** tie every tech to a requirement/number plus "what I didn't pick".
- **Ignored/vague NFRs:** write NFRs as numbers and link every decision to them.
- **No or random deep dives:** announce "I'll deep dive on X, Y, Z", each tied to an NFR.
- **SPOFs:** raise "what if this fails?" yourself, before being asked.
- **Silence:** draw while talking; the interviewer evaluates your thinking.
- **Time:** 45-min plan (req 5, entities 2, API 5, HLD 10–15, deep dives 10–15, wrap-up 3), watch the clock.
- **Arguing:** a hint is a gift; say "good point" and weigh the trade-off.
- **Over-estimation:** only numbers that change a decision.
- **Green flags:** drive the interview, state trade-offs, decide by numbers, name the hard part, raise failures, evolve simple → complex, close with improvements.

**Say in the interview:** "Before designing I'd like 2–3 minutes to clarify requirements. Now the HLD is up, I think the hard part is X, so I'll deep dive there first; tell me if you want another area."

**Avoid:** Hunting for a single "right answer", or practicing without speaking aloud and a timer. Adding a new component in the last 5 minutes with no wrap-up.
