**In one line:** A behavioral answer is one real story told as STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) in 60-120 seconds.

- **STAR time split:** S ~15%, T ~10%, A ~55-60%, R ~15-20%; Action is the biggest part.
- **"I" not "We":** "we" is fine in the Situation, the Action must be "I" (what you did and why).
- **Result:** give a number (latency, cost, hours saved), then one learning line: "Since then I always do X."
- **Steps:** one-line headline, S+T in 2 lines, A as 3-5 bullets, R as 1-2 numbers plus a learning.
- **Practice:** say it out loud with a timer; over 2 minutes means cut the Situation.
- **Follow-ups:** ask a friend for 3 probing questions; fake stories get caught on the 2nd or 3rd probe.
- **Story count:** keep 6-8 different stories, not one for every question.
- **Remember:** bullets, not a script.
- **Key question types:** conflict, failure, deadline, ownership, incident; all are "Tell me about a time...".

**Say in the interview:** "Let me give you a specific example. The context was... My specific role was..."

**Avoid:** Hypothetical ("I would...") answers, a long Situation, "we" throughout, no number in the result, blaming PM or QA.
