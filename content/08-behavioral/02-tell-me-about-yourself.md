# Tell Me About Yourself

**Ek line me:** ye interview ka pehla sawal hai aur poore round ka tone set karta hai. Ye resume padhna nahi hai, ye 60–90 sec ka pitch hai ki tum is role ke liye kyun fit ho.

> **Example:** "Main Lucknow se hoon, 2019 me B.Tech kiya, phir TCS join kiya, phir..." ye biography hai. Iske bajaye: "Main 3 saal se Swiggy-scale ke backend systems pe kaam kar raha hoon, abhi order tracking service own karta hoon jo 50K RPS handle karti hai..." ye pitch hai. Pehle wale me interviewer bore hota hai, doosre me woh follow-up sawal likhne lagta hai.

## ⭐ Interviewer kya check karta hai

- **Communication:** structured, crisp bol pate ho ya nahi.
- **Relevance:** tumhara experience is role se kaise juda hai.
- **Hooks:** kaunse topics pe aage sawal poochhe. Tum khud decide karte ho ki conversation kahan jaaye.
- **Motivation:** ye role kyun chahiye, sirf naukri ke liye ya genuine reason.

## ⭐ Structure: Present → Past → Why this role

| Part | Kya bolna hai | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **Present** | Abhi kya karte ho: role, company, team, scale, 1 highlight | 20–30 sec |
| **Past** | 1–2 pichhle experiences/projects jo is role se relevant hain, ek number ke saath | 20–30 sec |
| **Why this role** | Ye role/company kyun, aur tum kya laoge | 15–20 sec |

- Total **60–90 sec**. 2 min se zyada kabhi nahi.
- End clear signal ke saath karo ("...isliye main is role ke liye excited hoon"), taaki interviewer ko pata chale tumne baat khatam ki.
- Ek **hook** zaroor daalo: ek interesting project ya number, jispe interviewer poochhe.

## ⭐ Template 1: Fresher / final year

**Structure:** Present (college + focus) → Past (1–2 projects/internship) → Why this role.

**Fill-in:**
> "Main [college] se [branch] final year me hoon. Mera focus [backend / DSA / ML] pe raha hai. [Internship/project] me maine [kya banaya], jo [number/impact]. Iske alawa [ek aur project/achievement]. Mujhe [company] ka [product/tech] isliye interesting lagta hai kyunki [specific reason], aur yahan main [kya seekhna/karna chahta hoon]."

**Example:**
> "Main NIT Trichy se Computer Science final year me hoon. Mera main interest backend aur distributed systems me hai. Pichhli summer maine Razorpay me 2 mahine ki internship ki, jahan maine settlement reports ke liye ek async export service banayi. Pehle 1 lakh rows ka report 4 minute me timeout ho jaata tha, maine use queue-based banaya aur ab woh 40 sec me email ho jaata hai. College me maine ek Go me chhota key-value store banaya, Raft ke saath, jo GitHub pe 200 stars tak gaya. Aapki team high-throughput payment APIs pe kaam karti hai, aur main exactly isi tarah ke scale aur reliability problems pe kaam karna chahta hoon."

## ⭐ Template 2: 2–4 saal experience (SDE-1 → SDE-2)

**Structure:** Present (role + ownership + scale) → Past (ek strong project, numbers) → Why this role (growth, scope).

**Fill-in:**
> "Main [X] saal se [company] me [role] hoon, [team] me. Abhi main [service/area] own karta hoon jo [scale]. Mera sabse impactful kaam [project] tha, jahan maine [action] kiya aur [result number]. Usse pehle [previous role/company] me [1 line]. Ab main [bigger scope / domain] me jaana chahta hoon, aur [company] me [specific reason] isliye ye role mujhe right fit lagta hai."

**Example:**
> "Main 3 saal se Meesho me backend engineer hoon, catalog team me. Abhi main product listing service own karta hoon, jo sale ke din 30K RPS tak jaati hai. Mera sabse bada kaam pichhle saal tha: humara product search MySQL LIKE queries pe tha aur p99 2 second tha. Maine Elasticsearch migration lead kiya, dual-write aur shadow reads ke saath, zero downtime, aur p99 250 ms pe aa gaya. Usse pehle 1 saal ek startup me tha jahan full-stack kaam kiya. Ab main payments jaise domain me jaana chahta hoon jahan correctness aur scale dono matter karte hain, aur Razorpay ka scale aur engineering culture isliye mujhe attract karta hai."

## ⭐ Template 3: 5+ saal (Senior / SDE-3)

**Structure:** Present (scope, team, decisions) → Past (career theme, 1–2 big outcomes) → Why this role (impact jo laoge).

**Fill-in:**
> "Mere paas [X] saal ka experience hai, mostly [theme: platform / payments / infra]. Abhi main [company] me [role] hoon, [N] engineers ki team ke saath [area] ki technical direction set karta hoon. Pichhle 2 saal me [bada outcome with number]. Usse pehle [company] me [ek aur outcome]. Main ab [type of problem] solve karna chahta hoon, aur [company] me [specific] ki wajah se main [kya contribute karunga]."

**Example:**
> "Mere paas 7 saal ka experience hai, zyada tar high-scale backend aur platform me. Abhi main Flipkart me senior engineer hoon, 6 logon ki team ke saath order management ka technical lead hoon. Pichhle saal Big Billion Days ke liye maine order pipeline ko monolith se event-driven banaya, jisse peak 1.2 lakh orders/min bina incident ke chale, aur on-call pages 60% kam hue. Usse pehle 3 saal Paytm me wallet ledger pe kaam kiya. Main ab ek aisi team me jaana chahta hoon jahan platform zero se banana hai, aur aapka naya lending platform exactly wahi stage pe hai, jahan mera scale aur correctness ka experience seedha kaam aayega."

## Format ke hisaab se adjust karo

| Round | Kya emphasize karo |
|---|---|
| Recruiter / HR | Short (45–60 sec), motivation, notice period ka hint |
| Hiring manager | Ownership, scope, team fit, why this team |
| Technical (DSA/HLD) | 30–45 sec, tech depth, phir jaldi problem pe jao |
| Bar raiser / culture | Ek story jo values dikhaye (ownership, customer) |

## ⭐ Kya avoid karna hai

- **Resume padhna:** "2016 me 10th, 2018 me 12th..." Interviewer ke paas resume hai.
- **Personal life details:** shaadi, ghar, hobbies (jab tak relevant na ho ya poocha na jaaye).
- **Negative reason:** "Meri company me growth nahi hai, manager achha nahi..." Positive frame do: "zyada scope chahiye".
- **Bina number ke generic:** "Main hard-working aur passionate hoon." Har koi yahi bolta hai.
- **Bahut lamba:** 3 min ka monologue. Interviewer ka dhyaan 60 sec ke baad hatne lagta hai.
- **Buzzword list:** "Java, Spring, Kafka, Redis, Kubernetes, AWS..." Tech tab bolo jab kisi kaam se juda ho.
- **Company ke baare me kuch na pata hona:** "Why this role" part generic ho jaata hai.
- **Rata hua tone:** bullets yaad rakho, sentences nahi.

## Iske saath aane wale sawal

Ye sawal aksar intro ke turant baad aate hain. Inke jawab bhi 30–60 sec ke ready rakho.

### "Walk me through your resume"
- Same Present → Past structure, par **chronological** (purane se naye) chal sakta hai. Har job pe 1 line: kya kiya + 1 number + kyun aage badhe.
- Har switch ka reason positive ho: "zyada scope", "naya domain", "bada scale".

### "What are your strengths?"
- 2 strengths, har ek ke saath **proof**: "Production debugging. Pichhli sale pe 4 min me checkout incident mitigate kiya."
- Role se jodo: backend role me ownership, debugging, system thinking.

### "What is your weakness?"
- Real weakness lo jo role ke liye fatal na ho, aur batao tum kya kar rahe ho: "Main pehle bade PRs banata tha jo review me 3 din lete the. Ab stacked PRs (~300 lines) banata hoon, review time 1 din pe aa gaya."
- Avoid: "Main perfectionist hoon / bahut hard work karta hoon." Interviewer ise dodge samajhta hai.

### "Where do you see yourself in 5 years?"
- Growth jo company ke saath align ho: "Main ek aisa engineer banna chahta hoon jo poore system ka design own kare aur juniors ko mentor kare, senior/staff track pe."
- Avoid: "Apna startup kholna", "MBA karna", ya "pata nahi".

## Interview me bolo

> "Short me: main abhi [role] hoon jahan [scale wala kaam] karta hoon. Pehle [ek relevant highlight]. Aur main ye role isliye chahta hoon kyunki [specific reason]."

> End me: "Ye mera short background hai. Kisi part pe detail chahiye to main bata sakta hoon."

## Common galtiyan

- Har round me alag-alag version bolna jo resume se match na kare.
- "Why this role" part bhool jaana. Ye sabse zyada differentiate karta hai.
- Hook na dena, isliye interviewer random cheez pe sawal poochhta hai.
- Company ka research na karna (product, recent launch, tech blog).

## Checklist

- [ ] Present → Past → Why this role structure aur time split bata sakta hoon
- [ ] Apna "tell me about yourself" 60–90 sec me timer ke saath bol sakta hoon
- [ ] Apne pitch me kam se kam ek number aur ek hook de sakta hoon
- [ ] Recruiter, HM aur technical round ke liye version adjust kar sakta hoon
- [ ] Target company ke liye specific "why this role" line bol sakta hoon
- [ ] 5 cheezein bata sakta hoon jo is jawab me avoid karni hain
