# Amazon LPs & Company Values

**Ek line me:** har badi company behavioral round me apni values ke against score karti hai. Amazon ye sabse explicitly karta hai (Leadership Principles), par Google, Meta, Microsoft aur Indian product companies bhi yahi karti hain, bas naam alag hain.

> **Example:** Amazon SDE loop me 4–5 rounds hote hain, aur har round me DSA/design ke saath 1–2 LP sawal hote hain. Har interviewer ko 2–3 LPs assign hote hain. Coding perfect ho par LP stories weak hon, to offer nahi aata. Ye "soft round" nahi hai.

## ⭐ Amazon ke 16 Leadership Principles

⭐ = SDE interviews me sabse zyada poochhe jaate hain.

| LP | Ek line me |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **Customer Obsession** | Customer se shuru karo aur peeche ki taraf kaam karo. Customer ka trust jeetna aur rakhna |
| ⭐ **Ownership** | Long-term socho, "ye mera kaam nahi" kabhi mat bolo. Poori company ki taraf se kaam karo |
| **Invent and Simplify** | Naye ideas lao aur cheezon ko simple banao |
| **Are Right, A Lot** | Achha judgment, aur apni beliefs ko disprove karne ki koshish |
| ⭐ **Learn and Be Curious** | Hamesha seekhte raho, naye areas explore karo |
| **Hire and Develop the Best** | Talent pehchano, logon ko mentor aur grow karo |
| **Insist on the Highest Standards** | Bar high rakho, defects aage mat jaane do |
| **Think Big** | Bada vision, bold direction |
| ⭐ **Bias for Action** | Speed matter karti hai. Reversible decisions jaldi lo, calculated risk lo |
| **Frugality** | Kam resources me zyada karo. Constraint se innovation |
| ⭐ **Earn Trust** | Dhyaan se suno, seedha bolo, apni galti maano, respect do |
| ⭐ **Dive Deep** | Details me jao, data pe shak karo jab anecdote alag bole. Koi kaam chhota nahi |
| ⭐ **Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit** | Respect ke saath disagree karo, decision hone ke baad poora commit karo |
| ⭐ **Deliver Results** | Key inputs pe focus, quality ke saath time pe deliver. Setback ke baad bhi |
| **Strive to be Earth's Best Employer** | Kaam ki jagah safe, inclusive aur grow karne wali banao |
| **Success and Scale Bring Broad Responsibility** | Apne decisions ke wider impact (society, environment) ki zimmedari |

## ⭐ Most-asked LPs: sawal aur kya dikhana hai

### ⭐ Customer Obsession
- **Sawal:** "Tell me about a time you went above and beyond for a customer." / "A time you used customer feedback to change something."
- **Dikhao:** customer = end user ya internal team bhi. Tumne unki pain kaise measure ki (tickets, metrics) aur kya badla.

### ⭐ Ownership
- **Sawal:** "Tell me about a time you took on something outside your area." / "A time you made a sacrifice for long-term over short-term."
- **Dikhao:** problem dekhi, owner nahi tha, tumne uthaya, end tak le gaye. Long-term fix, sirf patch nahi.

### ⭐ Dive Deep
- **Sawal:** "Tell me about a time you solved a problem by going deep into data." / "A time metrics said one thing but your gut said another."
- **Dikhao:** logs, traces, query plans, numbers. Root cause tak kaise pahuche, 2–3 hypotheses jo reject ki.

### ⭐ Bias for Action
- **Sawal:** "Tell me about a time you made a decision without complete information."
- **Dikhao:** risk ka calculation, reversible decision, rollback plan. Wait karne ki cost kya thi.

### ⭐ Deliver Results
- **Sawal:** "Tell me about a time you delivered under a tight deadline." / "A time you hit a major obstacle."
- **Dikhao:** goal, obstacle, prioritisation, final number. Scope cut kiya to bhi core result deliver.

### ⭐ Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit
- **Sawal:** "Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager or team." / "A time you committed to a decision you didn't agree with."
- **Dikhao:** data ke saath push back, respect, aur decision ke baad poora commit (bina "maine to pehle hi bola tha").

### ⭐ Earn Trust
- **Sawal:** "Tell me about a time you had to deliver bad news." / "A time you admitted a mistake."
- **Dikhao:** transparency, jaldi batana, galti maanna, relationship repair.

### ⭐ Learn and Be Curious
- **Sawal:** "Tell me about a time you learned a new technology quickly." / "What's something you learned recently on your own?"
- **Dikhao:** structured learning, apply kiya, team ke saath share kiya.

## Amazon loop ka format

- **Rounds:** 4–5, har round ~60 min: ~15–20 min LP + ~40 min coding/design.
- **Bar raiser:** doosri team ka trained interviewer, hiring bar maintain karta hai. LP pe sabse zyada probe karta hai, ek story pe 15 min.
- **Story repeat mat karo** alag rounds me. Interviewers notes share karte hain.
- **Format:** STAR, "I" pe focus, data. Dekho [STAR Method](../08-behavioral/01-star-method.md).

## Google: Googleyness aur GCA

| Signal | Matlab | Kaise test hota hai |
|---|---|---|
| **GCA** (General Cognitive Ability) | Naye problem ko structure karke sochna | Open-ended / hypothetical: "Agar aapko X karna ho to kaise karoge?" |
| **Googleyness** | Ambiguity me comfortable, humble, collaborative, user focus, sahi kaam karna | Past behaviour + hypothetical: "Teammate deadline miss kar raha hai, kya karoge?" |
| **Leadership** | Bina title ke influence | "A time you led without authority" |
| **Role-related knowledge** | Tech depth | Coding / design rounds |

- Google me **hypothetical** sawal bhi aate hain. Jawab do, phir bolo "mere saath aisa hua tha..." aur real story se back karo.
- Hiring committee written feedback padhti hai, isliye clear, structured jawab matter karte hain.

## Meta: behavioral round

- Ek dedicated behavioral round hota hai (SDE ke liye bhi). Focus areas: **conflict resolution, ambiguity me kaam, growth (feedback lena), driving results, collaboration**.
- Values: Move Fast, Focus on Long-Term Impact, Build Awesome Things, Be Direct and Respect Your Colleagues.
- Level (E3/E4/E5) ka faisla bhi isi round se hota hai: scope kitna bada tha (khud ka task → team → multiple teams).
- Tip: har story me scope clear karo: "Ye 3 teams ko affect karta tha".

## Microsoft: growth mindset

- Culture: **growth mindset** ("learn-it-all, not know-it-all"), customer obsession, collaboration (One Microsoft), diversity & inclusion.
- Sawal: "A time you failed and what you learned", "How did you handle feedback?", "A time you helped a teammate grow."
- Last round ("as appropriate", senior leader) aksar behavioral + motivation hota hai.
- Tip: failure stories me "maine kya seekha aur kya badla" pe zyada time.

## Indian product companies

| Company | Kya focus karte hain | Typical sawal |
|---|---|---|
| **Flipkart** | Ownership, scale pe delivery, HM round me project deep dive | "Apne best project ka architecture aur aapka role batao", "Sale ke time koi incident?" |
| **Swiggy / Zomato** | Speed, ownership, customer impact, ambiguity | "Vague requirement ko kaise ship kiya?", "Kisi metric ko kaise move kiya?" |
| **Razorpay / PhonePe / CRED** | Correctness, ownership, transparency, high bar | "Ek galti batao jo production me gayi", "Kisi decision pe push back kiya?" |
| **Startups (Series A–C)** | Generalist, bias for action, ownership beyond role | "Apne role se bahar kya kiya?", "Kam info me decision?" |

- Indian companies me **HM round** sabse important behavioral round hai. Usually project deep dive + 3–4 culture sawal + "why leaving".
- Founders/CTO round ho to motivation, long-term fit aur ownership pe zyada focus.

## ⭐ Ek story → multiple LPs

Har LP ke liye alag story nahi chahiye. Ek strong story ko 3–4 LPs pe use kar sakte ho, bas **emphasis badlo**.

**Story:** "Diwali sale pe checkout latency 6 sec gayi, maine coupon query ka full table scan pakda, feature flag se 4 min me mitigate kiya, aur postmortem me 3 permanent fixes ki."

| LP | Kis part pe zor do | Opening line |
|---|---|---|
| Dive Deep | Rollback se rule out, slow query log, query plan | "Main ek incident batata hoon jahan pehli hypothesis galat thi..." |
| Bias for Action | Cache-only flag ka quick decision, risk vs wait | "Root cause pata chalne se pehle mitigate karna tha..." |
| Ownership | Postmortem, coupon creation me query review process | "Fix ke baad maine ye ensure kiya ki dobara na ho..." |
| Customer Obsession | 82% payment success, users ka failed checkout | "Har minute me hazaron customers pay nahi kar pa rahe the..." |
| Earn Trust | Har 10 min stakeholder update, transparent RCA | "Incident ke time maine sabse pehle communication set kiya..." |

- Same round me ek story ek hi baar. Alag rounds ke liye alag story better.
- Isliye 6–8 stories kaafi hain. Mapping ke liye dekho [Story Bank](../08-behavioral/05-story-bank.md).

## Interview me bolo

> "Main ek example deta hoon jahan data ne meri pehli assumption galat sabit ki..." (Dive Deep / Are Right A Lot ke liye strong opening)

> "Maine apni concern data ke saath rakhi, par decision hone ke baad maine poori tarah commit kiya..." (Disagree and Commit ka core)

## Common galtiyan

- LPs ko "theory" samajh ke skip karna. Amazon me ye coding jitna weight rakhte hain.
- LP ka naam bolna ("this shows my Ownership") bina behaviour dikhaye. Behaviour dikhao, naam interviewer khud lagayega.
- Ek hi story har round me.
- Google ke hypothetical sawal pe sirf theory bolna, real example se back na karna.
- Company research ke bina "why this company" pe generic jawab.
