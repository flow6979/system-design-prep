# Story Bank & Questions to Ask

**Ek line me:** 6–8 achhi tarah taiyaar stories se 90% behavioral sawal cover ho jaate hain. Story bank ek table hai jo batata hai kaunsi story kis sawal ya LP pe chalegi, taaki interview me sochna na pade.

> **Example:** Interviewer ne poochha "A time you went beyond your role". Bina story bank ke tum 20 sec sochte ho aur kamzor story pakadte ho. Story bank ke saath tum turant bolte ho "CI pipeline 35 → 9 min wala", kyunki woh table me Ownership ke saamne likha hai.

## ⭐ Story bank template

Apni stories is table me likho (Notion/Sheet me). Har row ek story.

| # | Story (headline) | Sawal / LPs jo cover karti hai | Numbers | Follow-up ke liye ready |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Order DB MySQL → Postgres migration, zero downtime | Proudest project, Deliver Results, Dive Deep, Think Big | p99 800 → 120 ms, 40 crore rows, 0 downtime | 4 phases, 0.02% mismatch root cause, rollback plan |
| 2 | Diwali sale checkout incident | Production incident, Bias for Action, Customer Obsession, Earn Trust | 23 min impact, p99 6 s → normal in 4 min | Rollback kyun nahi chala, postmortem items |
| 3 | `CREATE INDEX` bina CONCURRENTLY | Mistake, Earn Trust, Insist on Highest Standards | 4 min timeout, 1,200 orders, linter ne 3 aur pakde | Linter rules, kaise communicate kiya |
| 4 | Feature flag service, adoption 1/4 | Biggest failure, Learn and Be Curious, Customer Obsession | 6 hafte late, 3 mahine me 4/4 teams | Dobara karta to kya alag |
| 5 | Node → Go rewrite pe manager se disagreement | Disagree with manager, Have Backbone, Frugality | CPU −55%, ₹1.5 lakh/month | Doc, agar manager na maante to |
| 6 | Retry logic pe teammate se conflict | Conflict, Earn Trust, Dive Deep | Failures 0.3%, recovery 2 min | Teammate ki concern kya thi |
| 7 | CI pipeline 35 → 9 min | Ownership beyond role, Invent and Simplify | 150 engineer-hours/week bache | Manager se time kaise liya |
| 8 | IPL contest 2 hafte me | Tight deadline, Deliver Results, Bias for Action | 3.2 lakh submits/min, 18 lakh users | Scope kya kaata, load test |

Extra columns (optional): **Kis company/round me use ki**, **Kis role me (SDE-1/2)**, **Kitne sec me bolti hai**.

## ⭐ 6–8 stories kaise chuno

Stories ko categories se socho, taaki saare sawal cover hon:

| Category | Kitni | Kaunse sawal cover hote hain |
|---|---|---|
| **Big technical win** (migration, scale, perf) | 1–2 | Proudest project, Deliver Results, Dive Deep, Think Big |
| **Production incident** | 1 | Incident, pressure, Bias for Action, Customer Obsession |
| **Failure / mistake** | 1–2 | Biggest failure, mistake, Earn Trust, growth mindset |
| **Conflict / disagreement** | 1–2 | Teammate conflict, manager disagreement, Backbone |
| **Ownership / initiative** | 1 | Beyond role, Ownership, Invent and Simplify |
| **Ambiguity / tight deadline** | 1 | Vague requirements, deadline, prioritisation |
| **Learning / mentoring** | 1 | Learn quickly, feedback, Hire and Develop |

**Steps:**
1. Pichhle 2–3 saal ke kaam ki list banao: projects, incidents, PRs, reviews, appraisal notes. 15–20 raw items.
2. Har item ke saath likho: mera role, ek number, kya mushkil tha. Jisme ye teeno nahi, use hatao.
3. Upar wali categories se 6–8 chuno. Har category kam se kam ek baar cover ho.
4. Har story ko STAR bullets me likho. Dekho [STAR Method](../08-behavioral/01-star-method.md).
5. Har story ke saamne 3–4 sawal/LPs map karo. Dekho [Amazon LPs](../08-behavioral/04-leadership-principles.md).
6. Gap check: [Common Questions](../08-behavioral/03-common-questions.md) ke 12 sawal pe jao, har ek ke liye story hai? Nahi to nayi dhoondho.
7. Har story zor se bolo, 60–120 sec, aur ek dost se follow-up karwao.

**Tips:**
- Recent stories (pichhle 2 saal) prefer karo. Fresher ho to internship, college project, hackathon, open source.
- Variety: sirf technical nahi, people stories bhi (conflict, feedback, mentoring).
- SDE-2+ ke liye kam se kam 2 stories jahan scope team ya multiple teams ka ho.
- Kisi ek story pe zyada depend mat karo. Ek round me ek story ek hi baar.

## ⭐ Interviewer se kya poochho (round ke hisaab se)

"Do you have any questions for me?" bhi score hota hai. "Nahi, sab clear hai" = missed chance. 2–3 sawal ready rakho.

### Hiring manager round
- "Team abhi kis sabse badi problem pe kaam kar rahi hai? Pehle 6 mahine me mujhse kya expect karenge?"
- "Is role me success kaise measure hota hai? Ek strong SDE-2 aur average SDE-2 me kya fark dikhta hai?"
- "Team ka on-call aur release process kaisa hai?"
- "Pichhle saal team ne kya ship kiya jis pe aapko sabse zyada garv hai?"

### Peer / technical round
- "Ek normal din kaisa hota hai? Coding vs meetings ka ratio?"
- "Code review aur design review ka process kya hai? Design doc kaun likhta hai?"
- "Tech debt ko kaise prioritize karte ho?"
- "Aapne yahan join karne ke baad sabse zyada kya seekha?"

### Bar raiser / senior leader round
- "Company/org agle 1–2 saal me kis direction me ja raha hai?"
- "Aapke hisaab se yahan successful engineers me common kya hota hai?"
- "Culture ki kaunsi cheez bahar se dikhti nahi par andar matter karti hai?"

**Avoid:** salary/leave pe sawal (ye recruiter se), jo website pe already likha hai, "Mera interview kaisa gaya?".

## Salary, notice period aur "why should we hire you"

### Expected salary
- Recruiter pehle poochhega. Pehle research karo (levels.fyi, AmbitionBox, dost).
- Range do, number nahi: "Market aur role ke hisaab se main ₹X–Y LPA range dekh raha hoon, par main poore package aur role ko dekh ke decide karunga."
- Current CTC poochhe to sach batao (background verification me check hota hai). Breakup (fixed, variable, ESOP) clear rakho.
- Offer aane ke baad negotiate karo, interview ke beech nahi.

### Notice period
- Sach batao: "Mera notice period 60 din hai. Main buyout ke liye company se baat kar sakta hoon / early release ki koshish karunga."
- Counter-offer pe: "Main apne decision pe clear hoon kyunki mera reason role aur growth hai, sirf paisa nahi." (Aur ye sach hona chahiye.)

### "Why should we hire you?"
30–45 sec, 3 points: **role ki zarurat + tumhara proof + motivation**.
> "Aapko is role me aisa engineer chahiye jo high-scale backend own kar sake. Maine 3 saal me 30K RPS wali service own ki aur ek zero-downtime migration lead ki. Main production incidents me calm rehta hoon, Diwali sale pe 4 min me mitigate kiya. Aur payments jaisa correctness-critical domain mujhe genuinely excite karta hai, isliye main jaldi impact de paunga."

## ⭐ Final-day checklist

**Ek din pehle:**
- Story bank ek baar padho, har story ka headline + number yaad karo (words nahi).
- "Tell me about yourself" timer ke saath 2 baar bolo. Dekho [Tell Me About Yourself](../08-behavioral/02-tell-me-about-yourself.md).
- Company research: product, recent launch/news, engineering blog, team ka kaam. "Why this company" ki 2 specific lines.
- Har round ke liye 2–3 sawal interviewer ke liye.
- Resume ki har line pe 2 min bol sakte ho, check karo.

**Interview ke din:**
- Online ho to camera, mic, internet, charger, shaant kamra. 10 min pehle join.
- Paani, kagaz-pen (notes ke liye, story headlines ki chhoti list).
- Har jawab 60–120 sec. Lamba ho raha ho to "Main aur detail de sakta hoon agar chahiye" bolke ruko.
- Sawal samajh na aaye to clarify karo: "Aap team conflict ki baat kar rahe hain ya manager ke saath?"
- Round ke baad 2 min me note karo kaunsi stories use ki, taaki agle round me repeat na ho.

## Interview me bolo

> "Iske liye main ek specific example deta hoon..." (sochne ka 2 sec ka time lene ke liye bhi chalega)

> "Kya main ek minute soch sakta hoon ki sabse relevant example kaunsa hai?" (bilkul theek hai, random story se achha)

## Common galtiyan

- Stories interview ke din sochna. Pressure me sabse weak story yaad aati hai.
- Saari stories ek hi project se. Interviewer ko range nahi dikhti.
- Numbers yaad na hona, ya har round me alag number bolna.
- "Any questions?" pe "nahi" bolna.
- Salary pe interview ke beech me ladna, ya current CTC galat batana.

## Checklist

- [ ] Apna 6–8 stories ka story bank table (story → LPs/sawal → numbers) bana sakta hoon
- [ ] Har category (win, incident, failure, conflict, ownership, ambiguity, learning) ke liye ek story bata sakta hoon
- [ ] Common 12 sawal ke against apni stories ka gap check kar sakta hoon
- [ ] HM, peer aur bar raiser round ke liye 2–2 sawal poochh sakta hoon
- [ ] Expected salary, notice period aur "why should we hire you" ka crisp jawab de sakta hoon
- [ ] Final-day checklist follow kar sakta hoon aur rounds ke beech story repeat se bach sakta hoon
