**Ek line:** "Tell me about yourself" resume reading nahi, 60-90 sec ka pitch hai: Present, Past, Why this role.

- **Structure:** Present (20-30s: role, scale, highlight), Past (20-30s: relevant work + number), Why this role (15-20s).
- **Length:** 60-90 sec, kabhi 2 min se zyada nahi.
- **Interviewer check karta hai:** communication, relevance, hooks (aage kya poochhe), motivation.
- **Hook:** ek interesting project ya number daalo jis par wo sawal poochhe.
- **Ending:** clear signal do: "...which is why I'm excited about this role."
- **Templates:** fresher, 2-4 years, 5+ years ke liye alag; seniority ke hisaab se scope badhao.
- **Positive framing:** leaving reason negative nahi; "I want more scope".
- **Tech list:** buzzword list nahi, tech ko kisi kaam se jodo.
- **Company research:** "why this role" specific rakho, generic nahi.

**Interview me bolo:** "Right now I'm a [role] working on [work at scale]. Before that, [one relevant highlight]. And I want this role because [specific reason]."

**Galti mat karna:** Class 10 se resume padhna, personal life details, "no growth at my company", 3 minute monologue, memorised tone.
