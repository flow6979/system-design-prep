**Ek line:** HLD me log knowledge ki kami se nahi, approach ki galtiyon se fail hote hain: yeh 10 red flags mat karo.

- **Requirements skip:** seedha design = galat problem. 3–5 min sawal, FR + NFR, out of scope.
- **Over-engineering:** 1K QPS pe 12 microservices. Simple v1 pehle, number maange tabhi evolve.
- **Buzzwords:** har tech ko requirement/number + "kya nahi chuna" se jodo.
- **NFR ignore/vague:** NFR numbers me likho, har decision usse jodo.
- **Deep dive nahi ya random:** khud bolo "X, Y, Z pe deep dive", har ek NFR se juda.
- **SPOF:** khud bolo "ye fail hua to?", interviewer ke poochne se pehle.
- **Silence:** bolte hue draw karo; interviewer thinking evaluate karta hai.
- **Time:** 45 min plan (req 5, entities 2, API 5, HLD 10–15, deep dive 10–15, wrap 3), ghadi dekho.
- **Behes:** hint = gift; "achha point", trade-off socho.
- **Over-estimation:** sirf decision badalne wale numbers.
- **Green flags:** driver seat, trade-offs, numbers se decide, hard part pehchano, failures khud uthao, simple → complex, end me improvements.

**Interview me bolo:** "Shuru karne se pehle 2–3 min requirements clarify karna chahunga. HLD ke baad: is problem ka hard part X hai, pehle uspe deep dive karunga, aap kahin aur jana chahein to bataiye."

**Galti mat karna:** Ek hi "sahi answer" dhoondhna, ya mock interview bina bole/timer ke. Last 5 min me naya component, bina wrap-up ke.
