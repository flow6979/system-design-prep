**Ek line:** 6-8 taiyaar stories 90% behavioral sawal cover karti hain; story bank batata hai kaunsi story kis sawal/LP pe lagegi.

- **Table columns:** headline, questions/LPs covered, numbers, follow-up ready points.
- **Picking:** pichle 2-3 saal ke 15-20 items likho; role, ek number, kya mushkil tha; jo teeno na ho, hata do.
- **Coverage:** 6-8 stories choose karo, har category kam se kam ek baar; har story pe 3-4 questions/LPs map karo.
- **Gap check:** 12 common questions chalo; kisi ka story nahi to nayi dhoondo.
- **Practice:** har story 60-120 sec mein bolo, dost se follow-ups pucwao.
- **Interviewer se pucho:** HM round mein 6-month expectation, peer round mein code/design review process, bar raiser mein culture.
- **Din se pehle:** story headline + number yaad, "tell me about yourself" 2 baar timer ke saath, 2 line "why this company".
- **Din par:** 10 min pehle join, pen-paper, har answer 60-120 sec, unclear sawal clarify karo.
- **Har round ke baad:** 2 min note karo kaunsi story use hui.

**Interview me bolo:** "I'd love to understand what success looks like in this role in the first six months."

**Galti mat karna:** "No questions, sab clear hai" bolna (ye bhi score hota hai); aur bina practice ke story sunana.
