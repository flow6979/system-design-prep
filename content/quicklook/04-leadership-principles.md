**Ek line:** Har badi company behavioral round apni values pe score karti hai (Amazon ke 16 LPs sabse explicit); ek story kai LPs cover kar sakti hai.

- **Amazon loop:** 4-5 rounds, har ek ~15-20 min LP + ~40 min coding/design.
- **Bar raiser:** doosri team ka trained interviewer, ek story pe ~15 min probe karta hai.
- **No repeats:** rounds mein story repeat mat karo, notes share hote hain.
- **Customer Obsession:** customer ka pain kaise measure kiya (tickets, metrics) aur kya badla.
- **Ownership:** jis problem ka owner nahi tha usse end tak liya; long-term fix.
- **Dive Deep:** logs, traces, numbers; 2-3 hypotheses jo reject kiye.
- **Bias for Action:** adhuri info pe reversible decision, rollback plan.
- **Disagree and Commit:** data se pushback, decision ke baad poora commitment, "I told you so" nahi.
- **Earn Trust:** bad news jaldi batao, galti maano, relationship repair karo.
- **Learn and Be Curious:** structured learning, apply kiya, team ko share kiya.
- **Other companies:** Google (Googleyness), Meta, Microsoft (growth mindset), Indian product companies; format STAR hi.

**Interview me bolo:** "I raised my concern with data, but once the decision was made I committed fully."

**Galti mat karna:** Har LP ke liye alag nayi story dhoondhna (ek story multiple LPs map karo) ya rounds mein same story repeat karna.
