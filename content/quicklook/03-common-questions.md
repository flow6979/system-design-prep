**Ek line:** 80% behavioral sawal in 12 questions ke variants hain; har ek ke liye ek real story aur uska "signal" ready rakho.

- **Conflict teammate:** signal collaboration; story design/code review disagreement.
- **Disagree with manager:** backbone + commit; data se pushback, phir poora commitment.
- **Biggest failure:** self-awareness; ek project jo galat gaya, aur kya seekha.
- **A mistake:** honesty + process fix; prod bug jo tumne introduce kiya.
- **Tight deadline:** prioritisation; scope cut karke core deliver kiya.
- **Ownership beyond role:** orphan problem uthaya aur end tak le gaye.
- **Ambiguous requirements:** clarity banayi; vague ticket se v1 ship.
- **Tough feedback:** growth; feedback jise apply kiya.
- **Production incident:** calm under pressure; mitigate, root cause, permanent fix, numbers.
- **Proudest project / learning quickly:** depth aur curiosity.
- **Why leaving / why us:** motivation; positive, company-specific, 30-45 sec.

**Interview me bolo:** "Can I take a minute to think of the most relevant example?" (random story se behtar hai).

**Galti mat karna:** Kisi sawal ke liye story na hona, ya ek hi story baar baar use karna; leaving reason mein blame.
