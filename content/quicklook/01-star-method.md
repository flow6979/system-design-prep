**Ek line:** Behavioral answer ek real story hoti hai, STAR skeleton mein: Situation, Task, Action, Result, 60-120 sec mein.

- **STAR time split:** S ~15%, T ~10%, A ~55-60%, R ~15-20%; Action sabse bada hissa hai.
- **"I" not "We":** Situation mein "we" chalta hai, Action mein "I" (kya tumne kiya aur kyun).
- **Result:** number do (latency, cost, hours saved), phir ek line learning: "Since then I always do X."
- **Steps:** one-line headline, S+T 2 lines, A 3-5 bullets, R 1-2 numbers + learning.
- **Practice:** timer ke saath bolo; 2 min se upar ho to Situation kaato.
- **Follow-ups:** dost se 3 probing questions pucwao; fake story 2nd-3rd probe pe pakdi jaati hai.
- **Story count:** 6-8 alag stories rakho, ek hi story har question pe nahi.
- **Yaad rakho:** bullets, script nahi.
- **Key question types:** conflict, failure, deadline, ownership, incident; sab "Tell me about a time..." hain.

**Interview me bolo:** "Let me give you a specific example. The context was... My specific role was..."

**Galti mat karna:** Hypothetical ("I would...") answer, lamba Situation, poora "we", result bina number ke, ya PM/QA ko blame karna.
